# Defence UAV Design and Development

## Project Overview

This project focuses on the 3D CAD design and development of a custom Defence Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV), undertaken during my internship at **Yudru Technologies Pvt. Ltd.**

The UAV was designed as an integrated assembly comprising four major sections: **Wings, Tail, Body, and Electronic Setup**. Using SolidWorks, I developed the drone design by working on these sections and integrating them into a complete UAV assembly. I also worked on the physical arrangement of the electronic setup within the overall design.

This project provided practical experience in mechanical component design, 3D CAD modelling, assembly integration, and UAV product development.

## UAV Design and Major Sections

### 1. Wings

The wings were developed as one of the four major sections of the UAV design.

**Work performed:**
- Developed the 3D CAD representation of the wing section.
- Incorporated the wings into the overall UAV assembly.
- Coordinated the wing section with the other major sections of the design.

### 2. Tail

The tail was designed as a separate major section and integrated into the complete UAV model.

**Work performed:**
- Developed the CAD representation of the tail section.
- Integrated the tail with the overall drone assembly.
- Coordinated its placement with the other designed sections.

### 3. Body

The body forms one of the principal sections of the UAV assembly and provides the main area for integrating the different sections of the design.

**Work performed:**
- Developed the 3D CAD representation of the body.
- Integrated the body with the wings and tail.
- Incorporated the electronic setup arrangement into the overall design.

### 4. Electronic Setup

The electronic setup was considered as a distinct section of the UAV design, with a focus on the physical arrangement of electronics.

**Work performed:**
- Designed the physical arrangement of the electronic setup.
- Considered the placement of the setup within the overall UAV design.
- Coordinated the electronic arrangement with the body and complete assembly.

*Note: This project description covers physical arrangement only; it does not claim electronic circuit design, wiring development, or functional testing.*

## Project Workflow

1. **Design Planning:** Divided the custom UAV into four major sections: wings, tail, body, and electronic setup.
2. **3D CAD Modelling:** Developed the corresponding design sections using SolidWorks.
3. **Electronic Layout:** Worked on the physical arrangement of the electronic setup.
4. **Assembly Integration:** Integrated the designed sections into a complete UAV assembly.
5. **Design Documentation:** Prepared CAD files and screenshots to document the project, where permitted.
